---

SatWiki Research is a public notebook on space technology. It is written for people who need to check a single launch date or who want to understand how a particular orbit or subsystem behaves.

The material is split three ways:

- **News** (`/news/`) records events and dates, with the evidence stated. Commentary stays out.
- **Space Systems** (`/space-systems/`) explains how things work, in long form with tables, formulas and diagrams, filed into series.
- **Tools** (`/tools/`) holds utilities that run in the browser, such as the TLE parser.

The encyclopedic coverage lives on the community wiki at [sat.huijiwiki.com](https://sat.huijiwiki.com/). This site complements it: entries there, launch news and technical write-ups here.

The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/sat-wiki/sat-wiki.github.io), built with Hugo and Blowfish. Issues pointing out errors are welcome.
